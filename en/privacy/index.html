Framkey · Privacy and data

Privacy Policy

Framkey only reads the photos you select and the camera details, date, and coordinates stored in those photos. It does not access your live location. Editing and watermarking happen on your device. Photos, avatars, and shooting details are not uploaded to the developer's servers. Saving requires permission to add photos to your library; the original is not changed. Shooting details appear in the exported image, so you can review them before sharing.

When you use a template that includes a place, the app sends the coordinates stored in the photo to Apple Maps to look up the city and country name in English. The photo itself is not uploaded for this lookup. An internet connection is required. If there is no location data or the lookup fails, the place is left blank. You can also enter or change it in Photo Information.

Your byline, caption, avatar, favorites, and export preferences are stored on your device and can be changed at any time. Imported photos are temporarily stored on the device. The app attempts to remove them when editing ends or an import is canceled; an unexpected exit or cleanup failure may leave temporary files behind. Clear Cache in Settings removes only unused temporary photo files. It does not affect photos in use, personal settings, or photos in your system library.

To provide the free save allowance, Framkey records the cumulative number of successful saves in the device's system keychain. This record contains no photo content. Clearing the cache does not remove it; it may remain after you delete and reinstall the app. Apple handles Pro purchases, renewals, and refunds. The app verifies Pro status using Apple purchase records, does not collect payment card details, and does not require an account.

Framkey has no ads, user tracking, or third-party analytics. If you choose Follow the Creator or Send Feedback, the app opens Xiaohongshu or your email app; any subsequent information is handled by that service. Feedback is used to reply and resolve issues. You can also contact the developer through Send Feedback with privacy questions.
