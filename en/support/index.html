Common questions

A photo will not open

Choose it again in the system photo picker. Framkey needs access to the selected original. A damaged photo or one the system cannot provide may not open.

Camera details are blank

Framkey reads camera, lens, exposure, and date details that are actually present in the original photo. Missing fields stay blank; sample values are not substituted.

A place name is missing

Place names use coordinates embedded in the selected photo, not live location. Apple Maps lookup needs internet access. If coordinates are missing, the network is unavailable, or the lookup fails, the place stays blank. You can also enter or change it in Shot Details.

Free save allowance

The free allowance is 20 successful photo saves in total. If a save batch starts while saves remain, the batch can finish; every photo saved successfully in it counts. A batch can include up to 12 photos.

Restore or manage Pro

No Framkey account is required. Open Framkey Pro in Settings to review the plans and restore purchases. Apple handles subscription management and refunds. Manage auto-renewal in your Apple Account subscription settings. The App Store shows the actual price at purchase.

Clear Cache

Clear Cache in Settings removes only unused temporary photo files. It does not delete originals in your library, photos being edited, personal settings, or the free-save count.
